# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in Pebble a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, neurotype, disability, body size, gender identity, sexual orientation,
ethnicity, or level of experience.

We build software for mental-health-sensitive users; the same care applies to each other.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Offering constructive feedback, not personal criticism
- Showing empathy toward other contributors and users

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Public or private insults or jokes at another’s expense
- Publishing private information without consent
- Any form of discrimination or exclusion

## Our Responsibilities
Maintainers will clarify standards, review contributions fairly,
and may remove comments or contributions that violate this code.

## Scope
This Code applies to all project spaces (GitHub, discussions, social channels).

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the maintainer  
**Lesley-Ann Fenwick (MaraschinoGirl)** at the contact email in the repository profile.  
All complaints will be reviewed and handled respectfully.
